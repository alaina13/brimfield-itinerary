Weekend Itinerary

Your Brimfield Plan

Saturday Sept 12 – Sunday Sept 13, from Wellsworth Motel, Southbridge
Secretary desk Pearl necklace Knick-knacks / art MTG / Pokémon cards Quilts
Saturday: New England Motel sits right next to Midway, Central Park, and Hertan's, so park there Saturday and walk to every stop that day.
Sunday: Auction Acres is well south of New England Motel, past Quaker Acres, May's, and Crystal Brook, so you'll drive down to it after the covered pavilions rather than walk.

Cash, $5–$20/day.

Saturday

The fresh-selection hunt
7:00 – 7:30 AM · Depart Wellsworth Motel
US-20 West from Southbridge, ~20–30 min. Traffic thickens fast near the Sturbridge/Brimfield line — leaving on time buys you closer parking.
8:00 – 10:30 AM · Hertan's Field — Weekend Warrior Show
Pearls Cards
Arrive at opening — a fresh wave of vendors sets up for the weekend, so inventory is unpicked. Good ground for vintage estate jewelry and collectible lots.
Verified: Weekend Warrior only runs the last weekend of each Brimfield show (May 16–17, Jul 18–19, Sept 12–13) — today's exactly right for it.
Walk over from New England Motel — it's right next door on the map, no need to move the car.
10:30 AM – 1:00 PM · Central Park Antique Mart & Midway Antique Market
Knick-knacks Cards
Central Park leans sports memorabilia, pennants, and retro pop-culture — good for son's room. Midway's eclectic booths are where vintage toys and stray card boxes turn up.
No field at Brimfield specializes in trading cards — treat both as "possible," not "guaranteed," and ask dealers directly.
Continue on foot from Hertan's — no need to touch the car either way until you're ready to head to New England Motel for lunch.
1:00 – 2:00 PM · Lunch + mid-day scout
Walk the main strip for food; take a first pass at heavy furniture on May's and New England Motel while you eat on the move.
2:00 – 5:00 PM · New England Motel & May's Antique Market Desk
Evaluate secretary desks (no hutch): check dimensions, ask if the piece splits in two for the ID.4, and negotiate. Buy today and get it loaded before you leave — don't haul furniture on Sunday's short window.
You're already home base here — the car's steps away, so ask about a loading pass as soon as you arrive and load the desk straight in if you buy it.

Sunday

2-hour quick-strike clearance
7:30 AM · Depart Wellsworth Motel
Leave early — today's window is short.
8:00 – 9:00 AM · New England Motel — covered pavilions Quilts
Head under the covered tents for textiles and Depression-era quilts while dealers are fresh and the linens are clean and dry.
Verified: New England Motel's three pavilions (75 dealers) open at 6am Wed–Sun, so you can start earlier than 8 if you want first pick.
Same lot as Saturday — pay again since there's no overnight parking, but it's the same spot, no hunting for a new one.
9:00 – 10:00 AM · Brimfield Auction Acres
Quilts Knick-knacks
Head down for the picker booths — cottage-style and "cutter" quilts, plus any last boy-ish bedroom decor.
Verified: Auction Acres only opens two days per show — this week that's its closing weekend, so today's one of your only two chances.
Not next door — the map shows it several fields south, past Quaker Acres, May's, and Crystal Brook. Drive down and use Auction Acres' own lot rather than walking the full stretch in your one-hour window.
10:00 AM · Head home
Beat the midday traffic peak and the afternoon vendor pack-out gridlock on Rte 20.

What this map corrects

New England Motel is right next to Midway, Central Park, and Hertan's — they're clustered together on the same short stretch of Route 20. That's a much shorter walk than the "about a mile" estimate on the itinerary page — parking once at New England Motel and walking to Saturday morning's stops is easier than expected.
Auction Acres is not across the street from New England Motel. Going south on Route 20, it's several fields further down — past Hertan's, Quaker Acres, May's Antique Market, and Crystal Brook. Budget real walking time (or a short drive) between them on Sunday, not a quick street-crossing.
Parking (marked P on the map) is genuinely spread field-by-field along the whole strip — Dealer's Choice, Green Acres, Heart O'The Mart, Central Park, Hertan's, Black Swan Meadows, New England Motel, Quaker Acres, near Auction Acres, and near the town center all show their own lots.
